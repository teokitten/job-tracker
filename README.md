# Job Tracker

![Job Tracker screenshot](screenshot.png)

A lightweight, privacy-first job application tracker that runs entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no server, no data sent anywhere.

**[Open the tracker →](https://teokitten.github.io/job-tracker)**

## What it does

Log every job application in one place – company, role, location, platform, status, date, and notes. Filter by status (Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, Saved), sort by date, company, status, or most recently updated, and search across all fields with Ctrl+K.

For jobs you're interviewing with, track each interview round separately: date and time, type (phone screen, technical, onsite, final round, or online with a clickable meeting link), attendees, and notes. Mark a round as passed or failed – marking one failed automatically moves the job to Rejected. Round history stays attached to the job even after the outcome, so you can look back at how far a process went before it ended.

Platform entries link directly to the job listing when a URL is saved. Import or export your data as CSV, or print a clean PDF for reporting job search activity (e.g. to an unemployment office) – the PDF leaves out the Notes column so it doesn't run to dozens of pages, while CSV always keeps everything.

The app ships with a few sample jobs so you can see how it works before adding your own. Click **Clear sample jobs** to remove them without touching anything you've added yourself.

Your data stays in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere. To back it up or use it offline, click **Download tracker** – it saves a working copy of the whole app with your data embedded (Shift-click to include the sample jobs too, e.g. for sharing a demo).

## Tech

Single HTML file. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step.
